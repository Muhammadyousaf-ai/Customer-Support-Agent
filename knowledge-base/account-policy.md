# Identity verification
<!-- tiers: all; contracts: all -->

Before sharing subscription, contract or payment information, or making any change, the customer must be verified with the email on file and a six digit one-time verification code. After five failed attempts in a session, verification is locked and the case is escalated. Never disclose account details to an unverified customer.

# Updating account details
<!-- tiers: all; contracts: all -->

Verified customers may update their display name and notification email. Changes to the login email, password or two-factor settings must be done by the customer in account settings and cannot be made by support.

# Enterprise billing contact changes
<!-- tiers: enterprise; contracts: all -->

Changing the billing email or billing contact on an Enterprise account requires human approval from the account manager. Support agents must create an escalation ticket instead of editing the field.

# Team seats
<!-- tiers: pro, enterprise; contracts: all -->

Pro accounts include up to 10 seats and Enterprise accounts have unlimited seats. Seat changes are made by the account owner in team settings. Basic and Free plans do not support team seats.

# Deleting an account
<!-- tiers: all; contracts: all -->

Account deletion is permanent and removes all projects and data after 30 days. Deletion requests must be confirmed by the verified account owner and are handled by a human agent.

# Suspended and expired accounts
<!-- tiers: all; contracts: all -->

Suspended accounts cannot self-serve: every account change and refund request on a suspended account must be escalated to a human agent. Customers with an expired subscription or contract can still be helped with payments made inside the normal refund window, but cannot change plan settings until they renew.
