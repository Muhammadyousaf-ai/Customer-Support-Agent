# Payment methods
<!-- tiers: all; contracts: all -->

We accept major credit and debit cards. Payment method changes are made by the customer in billing settings. Support cannot read or store full card numbers. Enterprise accounts are invoiced under their contract instead of being charged to a card (see Enterprise invoicing).

# Invoices
<!-- tiers: basic, pro, enterprise; contracts: all -->

Invoices are generated on every payment and are available in billing settings as PDF downloads. Free plan accounts do not receive invoices because they are not charged.

# Failed payments
<!-- tiers: basic, pro; contracts: monthly -->

When a monthly payment fails, the charge is retried three times over seven days and the customer is emailed after each attempt. If every retry fails the account is downgraded to the Free plan. Updating the payment method triggers an immediate retry.

# Enterprise invoicing
<!-- tiers: enterprise; contracts: all -->

Enterprise accounts are invoiced on net-30 terms according to the contract rather than charged to a card. Payment disputes and invoice corrections are handled by the account manager.

# Duplicate or unexpected charges
<!-- tiers: all; contracts: all -->

If a customer reports being charged twice or charged unexpectedly, verify their identity, review the payment history, and escalate to the billing team. Support agents cannot reverse charges directly.
