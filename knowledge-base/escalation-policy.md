# When human intervention is required
<!-- tiers: all; contracts: all -->

Escalate to a human agent when the customer asks for something outside documented policy, when the policy does not clearly cover the request, when a refund exceeds automated limits or needs approval, when identity cannot be verified, when the customer is frustrated or asks for a person, and after repeated tool or system failures. The agent must create an escalation ticket with a summary of the conversation.

# Escalation response times
<!-- tiers: all; contracts: all -->

Escalated tickets receive a first response within two business days for Free, one business day for Basic, four hours for Pro, and one hour for Enterprise urgent issues.

# Enterprise escalation path
<!-- tiers: enterprise; contracts: all -->

Enterprise escalations are routed to the named account manager first and to the on-call support lead if the account manager does not respond within the response target.
