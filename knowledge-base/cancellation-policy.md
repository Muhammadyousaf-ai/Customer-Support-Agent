# Cancelling a monthly subscription
<!-- tiers: free, basic, pro; contracts: monthly -->

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from account settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal and the plan stays active until the end of the paid period. Cancelling does not trigger a refund by itself; refund eligibility is decided separately under the refund policy.

# Cancelling an annual subscription
<!-- tiers: basic, pro; contracts: annual -->

Annual subscriptions run for the full twelve month term. Cancelling stops auto-renewal but the plan remains active until the term ends, and no mid-term refund is issued automatically. Early termination with a refund or credit needs human approval from the billing team.

# Enterprise contract termination
<!-- tiers: enterprise; contracts: all -->

Enterprise contracts end according to the signed agreement. Termination requires 60 days written notice to the account manager. Self-service cancellation is not available for Enterprise accounts, and support agents must route cancellation requests to the account manager.
