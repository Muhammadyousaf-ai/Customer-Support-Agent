# General refund rules
<!-- tiers: all; contracts: all -->

Refunds are returned to the original payment method and typically appear within 5 to 10 business days. A payment that has already been refunded cannot be refunded again, and partial refunds are not supported. The standard refund window is seven days from the payment date. The maximum automated refund is $100 per payment; anything above that amount needs human approval. Free plan accounts are never charged, so there is nothing to refund.

# Monthly subscription refunds
<!-- tiers: basic, pro; contracts: monthly -->

Monthly Basic and Pro subscriptions can be refunded within seven days of the payment date. Refunds of $100 or less are approved automatically once the customer's identity is verified and the payment has not been refunded before. Refunds above $100 require human approval. After seven days the payment is not refundable, but the customer may cancel to stop future renewals.

# Annual subscription refunds
<!-- tiers: basic, pro; contracts: annual -->

Annual Basic and Pro subscription refunds always require human approval, regardless of the amount or how recently the payment was made. The support agent must create an escalation ticket for the billing team and must not issue the refund itself. Requests made within 14 days of the payment date are eligible for a full refund review; later requests are reviewed for a pro-rated credit.

# Enterprise refund exceptions
<!-- tiers: enterprise; contracts: all -->

Enterprise refunds are governed by the signed contract, which takes precedence over the standard policy. The standard seven day window does not apply: the default refund window for Enterprise is 30 days from the invoice date. Enterprise refunds are never automated. Every Enterprise refund request, regardless of amount, must be escalated to the customer's account manager for approval.
